Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our sober living community.

What is the cost of living at Premiere Sober Living?
Costs vary depending on the specific house and room type. We work to keep rates affordable while maintaining high-quality facilities. Please contact us for specific pricing information.
Are you a certified sober living facility?
Yes, we are officially certified through MHACBO and are a NARR (National Alliance for Recovery Residences) certified provider, ensuring the highest standards of care and safety.
What are the house rules?
Rules include mandatory house meetings, regular drug testing, adherence to curfews, and participation in a recovery program. A full list of rules is provided during the intake process.
Do you provide treatment programs?
We provide transitional housing and comprehensive support services. While we do not provide direct medical treatment, we have strong roots in the local recovery community and can connect you with outpatient programs.

Ready to Take the Next Step?

Our team is available 24/7 to answer your questions and guide you through the admissions process. You don't have to do this alone.
